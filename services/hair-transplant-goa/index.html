Hair Transplant
in Goa

A hair transplant moves healthy hair follicles from a donor area, usually the back of the scalp, to thinning or bald areas. Dr. Patel performs both FUE (individual follicle extraction) and FUT (strip method) and recommends the technique after assessing your hair loss pattern, donor supply and goals.

Dr. Patel

“Every hair transplant plan starts with an honest conversation about what will suit you.”

Book consultation

Who it is for

  • Pattern hair loss that has stabilised and has not responded well to medication
  • Receding hairline or thinning crown with a healthy donor area
  • Scarring or patchy loss from injury, burns or previous surgery

What to expect

  1. 01

    FUE

    Follicles are extracted one by one, leaving tiny dot-like marks that are easy to hide in short hair.

  2. 02

    FUT

    A thin strip is taken from the donor area and divided into grafts, which can yield a larger number of grafts in a single session.

  3. 03

    Planning

    Hairline design, graft count and density are agreed with you before surgery, so the result looks age-appropriate and natural.

Consultation first. Suitability, risks, alternatives and cost are discussed with Dr. Patel in person. Individual results vary.

What is the difference between FUE and FUT?

FUE extracts follicles individually with a small punch and leaves no linear scar. FUT removes a thin strip of scalp, which leaves a fine linear scar but can allow more grafts in one sitting. The right choice depends on your donor hair, hairstyle preference and the extent of loss.

When do transplanted hairs start to grow?

Transplanted hairs usually shed in the first weeks and new growth begins over the following months, with gradual improvement continuing through the first year. Timelines vary from person to person.
