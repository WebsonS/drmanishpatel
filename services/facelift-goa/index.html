Facelift
in Goa

A facelift tightens deeper facial tissues and removes excess skin to soften jowls, deep folds and loose neck skin. The aim is a rested, natural look, not a pulled or “operated” one.

“Every facelift plan starts with an honest conversation about what will suit you, and what may not.”

Who is a facelift for?

  • Sagging cheeks, jowls or a loose neck
  • Deep folds that fillers no longer address
  • Good overall health and realistic expectations

What happens during a facelift?

  1. 01

    Consultation

    Skin quality, bone structure and the ageing pattern of your face are assessed.

  2. 02

    Procedure

    Underlying tissues are repositioned and excess skin removed, with incisions concealed along natural lines.

  3. 03

    Combination

    Can be paired with eyelid surgery or skin resurfacing for a harmonious result.

Recovery, risks and who it may not suit

How long is recovery?

Bruising and swelling are greatest in the first two weeks. Most people prefer about two weeks away from social commitments; final settling takes months.

What are the possible risks?

  • Bleeding under the skin (haematoma)
  • Temporary or, rarely, lasting weakness of facial nerve branches
  • Scarring around the ears
  • Numbness of the cheeks and ears
  • Hair loss near the incisions

Who may it not suit?

  • Smokers who cannot stop before and after surgery
  • Uncontrolled blood pressure or bleeding disorders
  • Concerns that skin treatments alone can address
Consultation first. Suitability, risks, alternatives and cost are discussed with Dr. Patel in person. No result can be guaranteed and outcomes differ between individuals.

How long do facelift results last?

A facelift lifts tissues that have descended, but the face keeps ageing afterwards. How long the change lasts varies with age, skin quality, sun exposure and smoking.

Facelift or non-surgical treatments?

Mild laxity can often be helped with injectables or lasers. Significant sagging usually needs surgery. We will tell you honestly which suits you.
