Non-surgical · Long-term hair reduction

Laser Hair Removal
in Goa

Laser hair removal uses light energy to damage hair follicles and reduce hair growth over a course of sessions, leaving skin smoother. Most areas need multiple sessions spaced several weeks apart.

Dr. Patel

“Every laser hair removal plan starts with an honest conversation about what will suit you, and what may not.”

Dr. Manish Patel · Goa Medical Council, Reg. No. awaiting confirmationAsk the clinic

Who it is for

  • Unwanted facial or body hair
  • Frequent shaving, waxing, ingrown hairs or razor irritation
  • Those wanting a lower-maintenance routine

What to expect

  1. 01

    Suitability

    Skin and hair type are assessed so the right laser settings are used.

  2. 02

    Course

    A series of sessions treats follicles in their active growth phase.

  3. 03

    Care

    Sun protection and a simple aftercare routine support results and skin comfort.

Recovery, risks and suitability

Recovery

No downtime is usually needed. Mild redness may last a few hours. Sun protection is needed between sessions.

Possible risks

  • Redness, swelling or discomfort
  • Lighter or darker patches of skin, usually temporary
  • Burns or blisters, uncommon
  • Hair reduction, not complete removal; some regrowth is common

May not suit you if

  • A recent tan
  • Grey, white or very fine light hair, which responds poorly
  • Some skin conditions and medicines that make skin light-sensitive
Consultation first. Suitability, risks, alternatives and cost are discussed with Dr. Patel in person. No result can be guaranteed and outcomes differ between individuals.

Answers

Laser Hair Removal: your questions.

Still unsure? Ask Dr. Patel’s team

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on the area, hair thickness and hormones. A course of several sessions is typical, and your plan is set at the first visit.

Is it painful?

Most people describe a snapping or warm sensation. Cooling is used for comfort.
