Tummy Tuck (Abdominoplasty)
in Goa

Abdominoplasty removes excess abdominal skin and fat and tightens separated abdominal muscles to restore a flatter, firmer midsection, commonly after pregnancy or major weight loss.

Dr. Patel

Who is a tummy tuck for?

  • Loose abdominal skin after weight loss or pregnancy
  • Separated abdominal muscles (diastasis recti)
  • Stable weight and no plans for future pregnancies

What happens during a tummy tuck?

  1. 01

    Planning

    Skin laxity, muscle separation and scar placement are assessed and explained.

  2. 02

    Surgery

    Muscles are tightened, excess skin removed and the contour refined, often with limited liposuction.

  3. 03

    Recovery

    Support garments and a gradual return to normal activity.

Recovery, risks and who it may not suit

How long is recovery?

Expect one to two weeks off work and about six weeks before strenuous activity. A compression garment is worn and you may walk slightly bent at first.

What are the possible risks?

  • A long scar across the lower abdomen
  • Fluid collection (seroma) and wound-healing problems
  • Numbness of the lower abdomen
  • Infection and bleeding
  • Rare but serious: blood clots in the legs or lungs

Who may it not suit?

  • Plans for future pregnancy
  • Weight that is still changing
  • Smoking that cannot be stopped

What affects the cost of a tummy tuck in Goa?

The cost is different for each person, because it depends on the plan agreed at the consultation. The main factors are:

  • A full or a mini tummy tuck
  • Whether the stomach muscles need to be repaired
  • Liposuction of the waist or flanks at the same time
  • Anaesthesia, hospital stay and compression garments

The clinic gives a written estimate once Dr. Patel has examined you and the plan is clear.

Answers

Tummy Tuck (Abdominoplasty): your questions.

Still unsure? Ask Dr. Patel’s team

Is a tummy tuck the same as liposuction?

No. Liposuction removes fat only. A tummy tuck also removes loose skin and repairs muscles, so it addresses problems liposuction cannot.

Where will the scar be?

Typically low on the abdomen, designed to sit below most underwear and swimwear lines.

Can a tummy tuck repair muscle separation after pregnancy?

Yes. Separation of the stomach muscles (diastasis recti) is common after pregnancy, and tightening them is often part of a tummy tuck.

Should I wait until I have finished having children?

It is usually advised. A later pregnancy can stretch the skin and muscles again and undo part of the result.

Can I have a tummy tuck after a C-section?

Yes. The tummy tuck scar sits low, near where a C-section scar usually is, so the old scar can often be removed or included in the new one.
