Rhinoplasty
in Goa

Rhinoplasty reshapes the nose to improve its appearance, its breathing function, or both. It is planned around facial balance, so the nose suits your face rather than looking over-corrected.

Dr. Patel

“Every rhinoplasty plan starts with an honest conversation about what will suit you, and what may not.”

Dr. Manish Patel · Goa Medical Council, Reg. No. awaiting confirmationAsk the clinic

Who it is for

  • A bump, wide bridge, drooping tip or asymmetry that bothers you
  • Nasal injury, or a deviated septum that affects breathing
  • Adults whose facial growth is complete

What to expect

  1. 01

    Assessment

    Facial proportions, skin thickness, nasal structure and breathing are examined together.

  2. 02

    Surgery

    Performed under anaesthesia; the technique (open or closed) is chosen for your anatomy and goals.

  3. 03

    Healing

    Swelling settles gradually. Final refinement can take many months, and Dr. Patel reviews you through the process.

Recovery, risks and suitability

Recovery

A splint is usually worn for about a week. Bruising settles over two to three weeks, while swelling, especially at the tip, takes many months to resolve fully.

Possible risks

  • Bleeding and infection
  • Breathing difficulty or a blocked feeling
  • Asymmetry or an unsatisfactory shape
  • Altered sensation of the nose
  • A second (revision) operation in some cases

May not suit you if

  • Faces that are still growing
  • Expectations of copying someone else’s nose
  • Some medical conditions that raise surgical risk
Consultation first. Suitability, risks, alternatives and cost are discussed with Dr. Patel in person. No result can be guaranteed and outcomes differ between individuals.

Answers

Rhinoplasty: your questions.

Still unsure? Ask Dr. Patel’s team

Can rhinoplasty also improve breathing?

Yes. When a deviated septum or internal narrowing is present, the airway can be corrected in the same operation as the cosmetic changes. This is assessed at consultation.

How long until I see the final shape?

Most visible swelling settles in the first few weeks, but fine swelling, especially at the nasal tip, can take many months to resolve fully.
