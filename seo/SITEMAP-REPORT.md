## Fixed

1. **Accurate `<lastmod>`.** The 20 non-article pages got the build date on every build, so their dates changed whether the page had changed or not. Google stops trusting `lastmod` when it is like that. The build now hashes each page's own content (the `<main>` element, without scripts) and keeps the previous date until that content actually changes. Dates are stored in `_src/lastmod.json`, which is committed. Articles never show a date earlier than their publish date.
2. **Matching dates in structured data.** `BlogPosting.dateModified` and `MedicalWebPage.lastReviewed` now use the same date. Before, `dateModified` always equalled the publish date (even after edits), and `lastReviewed` was always the build day.
3. **404 page canonical removed.** The noindex 404 page carried a canonical URL (`/404`) that does not exist.
