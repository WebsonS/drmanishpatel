Date: 20 Nov 2026. Timeline is in weeks from "W1", the first week after Phase 0 gates are cleared (registration number in place, redirects ready). Work in W-1 and W0 (pre-launch) is listed first. The 35 existing posts are already written (see `SITE-STRUCTURE.md` section 3); this calendar adds depth, new pages and the gaps.

## Rules for every item (NMC and quality)

1. Written for patients, reviewed and dated by Dr. Patel; byline shows qualifications and registration number.
2. No testimonials, reviews, before/after, patient media, superlatives ("best", "top", "leading"), guarantees, offers, discounts, or price figures. Cost content explains what determines cost only.
3. Every clinical claim backed by a named source (ISAPS, ASPS, IAAPS, NMC, WHO, PubMed review) in a References block.
4. Structure: Short answer (40 to 60 words), H2 per question, "questions to ask your surgeon", "when to seek urgent help", link to the hub page and one location page.
5. Each post gets 1 GBP post (education only, no offer), 1 FAQ addition where relevant, and a schema update (real dates).
6. Cadence after launch: 2 posts per week maximum (review capacity), 1 new or expanded treatment page per week in Phases 1 to 2.
