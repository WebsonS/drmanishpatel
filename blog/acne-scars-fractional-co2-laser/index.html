Fractional CO2 Laser for Acne Scars: How It Works

Short answerFractional CO2 laser treats acne scars by creating tiny controlled injuries in the skin that trigger collagen renewal. A course of sessions gradually smooths texture, though scars are improved rather than erased.

How it works

The laser delivers light in a grid of microscopic columns, leaving surrounding skin intact. The skin heals quickly and rebuilds collagen, which lifts and smooths depressed scars.

Who it suits

It is commonly used for pitted and rolling scars once active acne has settled. Skin type matters, so an assessment comes first.

What to expect

Redness, a warm sensation and some peeling are normal for a few days. Sunscreen and gentle skincare are important during healing.

How many sessions?

Most people need a course of sessions spaced weeks apart. Your plan depends on scar depth and how your skin responds.
