Liposuction or Tummy Tuck: Which Addresses Your Concern?

Short answerLiposuction removes stubborn fat but does not tighten loose skin or repair abdominal muscles. A tummy tuck (abdominoplasty) removes excess skin and fat and can tighten muscles separated by pregnancy. Some people need one, some the other, and some a combination.

Start with what is actually there

The tummy can look full for three different reasons: fat under the skin, loose or stretched skin, and a weakened or separated abdominal wall. Each needs a different approach, so the first step is an examination, not a choice of procedure.

Liposuction and tummy tuck compared
LiposuctionTummy tuck
What it removesFatLoose skin and fat
Tightens loose skinNo; relies on your skin shrinking backYes
Repairs separated musclesNoCan, if they have separated
ScarSmall incisionsA longer scar, usually placed low enough for underwear to cover
RecoveryUsually shorterLonger
Often suitsGood skin elasticity, localised fat, stable weightLoose skin after pregnancy or major weight loss

When liposuction may suit

  • Skin with good elasticity that can shrink back after fat is removed
  • Localised fat that has not responded to diet and exercise
  • A weight that has been stable for some time

Liposuction uses small incisions and a thin cannula. It reshapes; it is not a weight-loss treatment.

When a tummy tuck may suit

  • Loose, overhanging skin after pregnancy or major weight loss
  • Stretch marks below the navel that you would like removed with the skin
  • A bulge from separated abdominal muscles (diastasis recti)

A tummy tuck leaves a longer scar, usually placed low so underwear can cover it, and recovery takes longer than liposuction. Women who plan more pregnancies are usually advised to wait.

Recovery and risks

Both are surgical procedures. Swelling, bruising, fluid collection, changes in skin sensation, contour irregularity and scarring are possible, and smoking raises the risk of healing problems. The specific risks for your health and body are discussed at consultation, along with the recovery time you would need off work.
