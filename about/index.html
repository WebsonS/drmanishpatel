About the surgeon

Dr. Manish
Patel

Plastic and cosmetic surgeon with more than three decades of experience, practising in Margao and Panaji, Goa.

A surgeon’s job is as much about what he advises against as what he performs.

Dr. Manish Patel is a plastic surgeon in Goa, India, with clinics in Margao and Panaji. He holds MBBS, MS (General Surgery) and MCh (Plastic Surgery) degrees, is registered with the Goa Medical Council and has practised for more than 30 years. In 2011 he headed the Department of Cosmetic Surgery at the Wockhardt Institute of Aesthetics, and he has taught as faculty at IAAPS AESURG 2022 and as a Botox and fillers trainer at the 7th FADS World Congress 2023. His practice covers cosmetic and reconstructive surgery of the face, breast and body, hair transplant, and laser and skin treatments. He consults in English, Hindi and Konkani.

Registered with the Goa Medical Council

His approach is simple: understand what you want, be honest about what is and is not achievable, and explain the risks before anything is decided. He plans and performs your treatment personally, and remains reachable throughout recovery.
