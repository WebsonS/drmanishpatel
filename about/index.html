About the surgeon

Dr. Manish
Patel

Plastic and cosmetic surgeon with more than three decades of experience, practising in Margao and Panaji, Goa.

A surgeon’s job is as much about what he advises against as what he performs.

Dr. Manish Patel holds MBBS, MS (General Surgery) and MCh (Plastic Surgery) qualifications and has over 30 years of experience. His practice covers cosmetic and reconstructive surgery, hair restoration and skin treatments.

Registered with the Goa Medical Council

His approach is simple: understand what you want, be honest about what is and is not achievable, and explain the risks before anything is decided. He plans and performs your treatment personally, and remains reachable throughout recovery.
