Questions, answered.

Still unsure? Ask Dr. Patel’s team

What qualifications does Dr. Manish Patel have?

Dr. Manish Patel holds MBBS, MS (General Surgery) and MCh (Plastic Surgery). He is registered with the Goa Medical Council and has practised for more than 30 years.

Which procedures do you offer?

Surgical procedures include hair transplant (FUE and FUT), rhinoplasty, liposuction, breast augmentation, gynecomastia surgery, facelift and tummy tuck. Non-surgical and skin treatments include Botox, fillers, laser hair removal, acne scar treatment and skin rejuvenation.

How much do treatments cost?

Cost depends on the procedure, its extent and your individual plan, so a fair figure is only possible after an examination. You receive a written estimate after your consultation, with no obligation to go ahead.

How do I make an appointment?

Call the Margao clinic on +91 92845 62451 or the Panaji clinic on +91 97308 56787, message the clinic on WhatsApp, or send the enquiry form on the contact page.
