Patient informationHair Transplant

Recovery

Most people return to desk work within a few days. Scabs settle in about two weeks; transplanted hairs usually shed first, and new growth shows over several months.

Possible risks

  • Swelling of the forehead and temporary numbness of the scalp
  • Infection or folliculitis (inflamed follicles)
  • Visible donor scarring: fine dots with FUE, a linear scar with FUT
  • Temporary shedding of existing hair near the grafts
  • Lower graft growth or density than expected

May not suit you if

  • Hair loss that is still progressing rapidly
  • A donor area too thin to supply grafts
  • Some causes of hair loss, such as untreated alopecia areata

Patient informationLiposuction

Recovery

A compression garment is worn for several weeks. Light activity resumes within days; bruising and swelling settle over weeks to months.

Possible risks

  • Contour irregularities, dents or asymmetry
  • Fluid collection (seroma) or bleeding
  • Changes in skin sensation
  • Infection
  • Rare but serious: blood clots, fat embolism, injury to deeper structures

May not suit you if

  • Weight loss: it is not a substitute for it
  • Loose skin with poor elasticity
  • Unstable weight or significant medical conditions

Patient informationBreast Augmentation

Recovery

Most people need about a week away from work and several weeks before heavy lifting or exercise. A supportive bra is worn as advised.

Possible risks

  • Capsular contracture (hardening around the implant)
  • Implant rupture, rippling or displacement
  • Changes in nipple or breast sensation
  • Infection and bleeding
  • Further surgery over a lifetime; implants are not permanent devices

May not suit you if

  • Pregnancy or breastfeeding
  • Active infection or untreated breast disease
  • Unrealistic expectations of size or shape

Patient informationRhinoplasty

Recovery

A splint is usually worn for about a week. Bruising settles over two to three weeks, while swelling, especially at the tip, takes many months to resolve fully.

Possible risks

  • Bleeding and infection
  • Breathing difficulty or a blocked feeling
  • Asymmetry or an unsatisfactory shape
  • Altered sensation of the nose
  • A second (revision) operation in some cases

May not suit you if

  • Faces that are still growing
  • Expectations of copying someone else’s nose
  • Some medical conditions that raise surgical risk

Patient informationGynecomastia Surgery

Recovery

A compression vest is worn for several weeks. Desk work is usually possible within a week; exercise resumes gradually.

Possible risks

  • Contour irregularity or residual tissue
  • Fluid collection (seroma) or bleeding
  • Changes in nipple sensation
  • Visible scars
  • Infection

May not suit you if

  • Breast enlargement from a medicine or condition that has not been assessed
  • Teenagers whose tissue may still settle on its own
  • Ongoing anabolic steroid use

Patient informationFacelift

Recovery

Bruising and swelling are greatest in the first two weeks. Most people prefer about two weeks away from social commitments; final settling takes months.

Possible risks

  • Bleeding under the skin (haematoma)
  • Temporary or, rarely, lasting weakness of facial nerve branches
  • Scarring around the ears
  • Numbness of the cheeks and ears
  • Hair loss near the incisions

May not suit you if

  • Smokers who cannot stop before and after surgery
  • Uncontrolled blood pressure or bleeding disorders
  • Concerns that skin treatments alone can address

Patient informationBotox & Fillers

Recovery

Most people return to normal activities the same day. Small bruises or swelling at injection sites settle within days.

Possible risks

  • Bruising, swelling or tenderness
  • Temporary drooping of an eyelid or brow (Botox)
  • Lumps or asymmetry (fillers)
  • Rare but serious with fillers: a blocked blood vessel, which can damage skin or vision
  • Effects are temporary and wear off over months

May not suit you if

  • Pregnancy or breastfeeding
  • Infection at the treatment site
  • Certain neuromuscular conditions (Botox)

Patient informationTummy Tuck (Abdominoplasty)

Recovery

Expect one to two weeks off work and about six weeks before strenuous activity. A compression garment is worn and you may walk slightly bent at first.

Possible risks

  • A long scar across the lower abdomen
  • Fluid collection (seroma) and wound-healing problems
  • Numbness of the lower abdomen
  • Infection and bleeding
  • Rare but serious: blood clots in the legs or lungs

May not suit you if

  • Plans for future pregnancy
  • Weight that is still changing
  • Smoking that cannot be stopped
General information. Your own risks are assessed at consultation.Full guide
Patient informationLaser Hair Removal

Recovery

No downtime is usually needed. Mild redness may last a few hours. Sun protection is needed between sessions.

Possible risks

  • Redness, swelling or discomfort
  • Lighter or darker patches of skin, usually temporary
  • Burns or blisters, uncommon
  • Hair reduction, not complete removal; some regrowth is common

May not suit you if

  • A recent tan
  • Grey, white or very fine light hair, which responds poorly
  • Some skin conditions and medicines that make skin light-sensitive
General information. Your own risks are assessed at consultation.Full guide
Patient informationAcne Scar Treatment

Recovery

Redness, a sunburn-like feeling and peeling last several days. Strict sun protection is needed while the skin heals.

Possible risks

  • Prolonged redness
  • Darkening of the skin (post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation), more likely in darker skin
  • Infection or a flare of cold sores
  • Scars are improved, not erased

May not suit you if

  • Active acne
  • Recent isotretinoin use
  • A tendency to keloid scars
General information. Your own risks are assessed at consultation.Full guide
Patient informationSkin Rejuvenation & Pigmentation

Recovery

Depends on the treatment: from no downtime for gentle peels to several days of redness and peeling after stronger peels or lasers.

Possible risks

  • Irritation, redness or peeling
  • Pigmentation that worsens or returns, especially melasma
  • Sensitivity to sun
  • Results depend on continued sun protection and skincare

May not suit you if

  • Undiagnosed pigmented spots, which need examination first
  • Active skin infection
  • Pregnancy, for some treatments

Still unsure? Ask Dr. Patel’s team

What qualifications does Dr. Manish Patel have?

Dr. Manish Patel holds MBBS, MS (General Surgery) and MCh (Plastic Surgery). He is registered with the Goa Medical Council and has practised for more than 30 years.

Why are there no patient reviews or before-and-after photos on this website?

The National Medical Commission’s guidelines on ethical advertising do not allow doctors to publish patient testimonials, reviews or before-and-after results to promote their practice. At your consultation Dr. Patel explains what a procedure can and cannot change for you, including its risks.

Is plastic surgery safe?

Every surgical procedure carries risk, including bleeding, infection, scarring, anaesthetic complications and a result that differs from what you hoped for. Risk is reduced, not removed, by a full assessment, an equipped facility and careful aftercare. Each procedure’s specific risks are discussed with you before you decide.

How much do treatments cost?

Cost depends on the procedure, its extent and your individual plan, so a fair figure is only possible after an examination. You receive a written estimate after your consultation, with no obligation to go ahead.
