Still unsure? Ask Dr. Patel’s team

Who is the best plastic surgeon in Goa?

Choose a surgeon by verified training and experience, not by claims. Dr. Manish Patel is a plastic surgeon holding MBBS, MS (General Surgery) and MCh (Plastic Surgery) qualifications with over 30 years of experience, and consults at clinics in Margao and Panaji, Goa.

What qualifications does Dr. Manish Patel have?

Dr. Manish Patel holds MBBS, MS (General Surgery) and MCh (Plastic Surgery), the super-specialty degree in plastic surgery in India.

Where are the clinics located?

There are two clinics in Goa: Margao (near B101, CD Signature, Bolshe Cir, Borda, 403602) and Panaji (1st Floor, 112-A, Gerra Imperium Star, Patto, next to State Central Library, 403001).

Which procedures do you offer?

Surgical procedures include hair transplant (FUE and FUT), rhinoplasty, liposuction, breast augmentation, gynecomastia surgery, facelift and tummy tuck. Non-surgical and skin treatments include Botox, fillers, laser hair removal, acne scar treatment and skin rejuvenation.

How much do treatments cost?

Cost depends on the procedure, its extent and your individual plan, so a fair quote is only possible after a consultation. Book a consultation and you will receive a clear estimate before deciding.

How do I book a consultation?

Call the Margao clinic on +91 92845 62451 or the Panaji clinic on +91 97308 56787, message us on WhatsApp, or use the booking form on the contact page.

Is plastic surgery safe?

Surgery always carries some risk, which is reduced by choosing a qualified surgeon, a proper pre-operative assessment and good aftercare. Every procedure is discussed with you in detail, including its risks and alternatives.
